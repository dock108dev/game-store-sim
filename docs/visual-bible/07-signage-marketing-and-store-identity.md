Status: Active visual bible
Spreadsheet families: Signage, Marketing, Paper Goods, Store Brand rows
Primary IDs: OBJ-003, OBJ-011, OBJ-015, OBJ-049, OBJ-061, OBJ-072, OBJ-073, OBJ-084 through OBJ-086, OBJ-160 through OBJ-184, OBJ-203, OBJ-204

## Target Read

Signage should make the store legible without becoming visual noise. The current direction has repeatedly overused labels. The next store should rely on product art, fixture shape, layout, and materials first; signage should support the retail read, not explain every object.

## Locked Decisions

- `Games4U` remains the default store name.
- Store identity should be editable later.
- Signage must be readable.
- Day-one marketing should be minimal; one grand-opening sign may be enough.
- Posters should be swappable and tied to campaigns/release calendar later.
- Section headers should eventually be player-editable.
- Avoid wall posters until the store has real inventory.

## Day-One Signage Rule

Day-one store should include:

- exterior/interior `Games4U` sign
- maybe one grand-opening sign
- maybe small door hours/open sign
- case price stickers
- no broad promo wall
- no dense platform wall labels before inventory exists

The store should feel empty because it is new and understocked, not because signs are missing.

## Text Quality Rules

Readable text should be:

- bitmap/texture-based or clean mesh/sign asset
- high enough resolution for player distance
- legal-safe fictional copy
- used sparingly

Avoid:

- pixel-noise signs
- floating debug labels
- every shelf or area labeled because the art fails
- unreadable walls of tiny text

## Marketing Tone

Tone can be fun and lightly funny. It should not become a parody of a real retailer or real game campaign.

Safe examples:

- `Grand Opening`
- `Trade-ins coming soon`
- `New games weekly`
- `Footy 2002`
- `Critter Quest II`

Avoid direct riffs on real retailer slogans, game titles, logos, rating systems, or platform campaigns.

## Validation Shots

Required:

- storefront sign readability shot
- day-one interior shot showing minimal signage
- product case sticker close shot
- optional grand-opening sign shot

Pass criteria: signs are readable where intended, but the store does not depend on signage to look like a game shop.
