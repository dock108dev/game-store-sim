# Replay Junction — personal Mac review build

The B9 delivery record identifies the app and its hash. Open `artifacts/b9-personal-mac/Replay Junction Personal Beta.app` from this repository, or expand its adjacent `Replay Junction.zip` and open the app. No Godot editor is needed. This is a local, ad-hoc signed build; it is not notarized or approved for public distribution. Do not bypass an unexpected security warning: record what macOS says for this exact artifact.

Choose **New week** for a fresh shop or **Continue** for the last successful checkpoint. Help/F1 explains the controls and business rules. Click stations or the shift button to work, click clear floor to walk, or use WASD/arrows. Keyboard movement cancels the current trip; click the desired station again afterward. Use **Menu / Quit → Save and quit** to finish a session. Closing the window opens the same confirmation. Failed saves keep the session open with a retry option.

Seven in-game days include new releases on days 4 and 6, used-game sellers from day 2, staff from day 3 and the north bay from day 5. Rent is due at the final close. Finances distinguishes cash purchases, sold-copy cost, wages/rent and fixtures. Survival does not necessarily mean profit. Restart preserves the terminal result before beginning a new week.

The personal build uses `~/Library/Application Support/game-sim-personal-beta-v10/`. It does not read or migrate development or earlier owner saves. B9 synthetic tests are archived separately before handoff; no test shop should appear in the owner’s initial Continue slot. Schema 10 and the B6 retail rules are unchanged.

This build intentionally has no music or sound effects. Successful audio-device startup does not mean sound content was added.

## Known limits for the later B10 review

- Starting capital permits survival without selling, while losing operating value. Judge pressure and motivation across the full week.
- A second rack supports tested stocking volume. The tested north bay adds unused capacity; two employees can cost more than one without increasing tested checkout sales. These are optional decisions to judge, not promised returns.
- Individual labeling, shelving and seller-dialog work can feel repetitive. Negotiation is bounded and seller-only.
- Illustrated cutout turns, mirrored lighting, generic reaches, residual foot sliding and displaced/crowded labels remain.
- B9 repairs a reproduced traffic deadlock; bounded tests cannot prove every player-created layout and traffic pattern. If travel stops, record the day, location and nearby shoppers. WASD can cancel travel, but repeated detours should be reported as a navigation defect.
- This build is technically qualified only to the extent recorded in B9. Fun, comprehension, motion quality and personal-beta acceptance require the separate B10 owner review. B10 has not started.
