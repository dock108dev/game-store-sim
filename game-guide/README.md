# Game Store Sim Official Strategy Guide

Premium Edition

Welcome to the complete strategy guide for Game Store Sim. This guide covers the full road from an empty mall lease to a legendary independent game shop: every core system, every major unlock band, every customer type, every store-growth phase, and every secret-thread route worth replaying.

Game Store Sim is a retail life sim first. You succeed by running the shop: receiving stock, pricing games, stocking shelves, helping customers, managing launch days, choosing suppliers, protecting reputation, and deciding what kind of owner you want to be.

Secrets exist, but they hide inside the business. Suspicious offers, strange serial numbers, off-manifest stock, collector rumors, and supplier pressure all arrive through normal store work. The best players do not hunt for secrets outside the sim; they notice what the store is already telling them.

## One-Line Strategy

Treat every shelf as a promise. Empty space promises growth, fair prices promise trust, clean records promise safety, and risky shortcuts promise consequences.

## Completion Scale

Opening the store and making the first sale is only about 0.3% completion. The full game is a long-form store sim about building a business over years, surviving era changes, mastering customer trust, and resolving or ignoring optional secret threads across multiple replays.
