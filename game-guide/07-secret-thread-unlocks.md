# Secret Thread Unlocks

This is the full secret-thread route guide. Use it to plan replayable mystery runs without losing control of the store.

## Secret Thread Structure

Every secret thread has five stages:

1. Seed: a normal retail action creates a strange detail.
2. Pattern: a second or third detail shows it is not random.
3. Choice: the player records, ignores, sells, isolates, reports, rejects, or follows up.
4. Consequence: cash, reputation, supplier access, customer trust, inspection risk, and story state shift.
5. Resolution: after enough years/days and records, the thread closes or remains unresolved.

The player can run the store forever without completing these.

## Global Hidden States

Track these hidden-state scores:

- Evidence Count
- Clean Records
- Risky Sales
- Supplier Heat
- Customer Trust Damage
- Inspection Risk
- Collector Confidence
- Isolation Discipline

Secret endings read from these scores.

## Thread 1: North Dock, No Receipt

Theme: cheap used stock from a supplier who knows too much about cash buyers.

Seed unlock:

- Order or receive the Used Game Starter Lot from North Dock Wholesale.
- Read invoice language about count variance or quiet terms.

Pattern unlock:

- A supplier note appears.
- A suspicious contact asks for bulk Orbit 64 copies, no receipts.
- A used item has a serial mismatch or suspicious risk tag.

Choices:

- Ignore the note and keep selling.
- Document the note.
- Reject the goods.
- Follow up with North Dock.
- Accept the cash buyer.
- Isolate the suspect stock.

Consequences:

- Follow-up supplier increases supplier access but may keep you tied to North Dock.
- Rejecting goods protects trust but may reduce access.
- Accepting cash gives short-term money and raises inspection risk.
- Documenting creates clean evidence and lowers risk.

Resolutions:

- Clean Break: reject/report enough North Dock anomalies and keep store trust high.
- Quiet Partnership: keep access and avoid direct reporting, but carry persistent inspection risk.
- Cash Trail: accept cash repeatedly and trigger a high-risk store identity ending.
- Unresolved: ignore details; North Dock remains a background supplier shadow.

Replay reason:

- This thread tests whether the player values cheap used inventory over clean records.

## Thread 2: The Serial Ghost

Theme: one product's metadata says it is not the item it claims to be.

Seed unlock:

- Inspect a mismatched serial item in receiving or trade-in intake.

Pattern unlock:

- Serial lookup shows expected and recorded IDs differ.
- Another item shares a suspicious provenance pattern.
- A customer asks about condition or authenticity.

Choices:

- Sell as normal.
- Document evidence.
- Isolate goods.
- Report issue.
- Ignore until later.

Consequences:

- Selling as normal increases inspection risk and lowers trust if discovered.
- Documenting or isolating protects the store.
- Reporting improves reputation but may reduce supplier access.

Resolutions:

- Authenticity Advocate: all serial mismatches documented or isolated.
- Willful Blindness: mismatches sold through as normal.
- Evidence Shelf: suspect goods preserved and records kept for later.
- Paper Gap: the player never resolves whether the serial was error, counterfeit, or theft.

Replay reason:

- This thread creates a clean-versus-cash decision at the item level.

## Thread 3: The Impossible Launch Copy

Theme: a product appears before it can plausibly exist.

Seed unlock:

- Reach release-calendar play.
- Receive a trade-in, supplier item, or collector comment about a title not yet launched.

Pattern unlock:

- Release calendar says the item is not released yet.
- Supplier invoice claims normal shipment timing.
- Collector recognizes the cover or asks how you got it.

Choices:

- Hold it in backroom isolation.
- Document and report.
- Follow up supplier.
- Sell quietly to a collector.
- Display it as a tease without selling.

Consequences:

- Selling early creates cash and collector buzz but raises inspection and trust risk.
- Holding it protects the store but costs short-term opportunity.
- Reporting may burn supplier access but improve reputation.

Resolutions:

- Street-Date Saint: never sells early stock.
- Hype Dealer: uses impossible copies to build cash and buzz.
- Supplier Leak: traces the source through supplier follow-ups.
- Collector Covenant: privately protects a collector relationship without broad sale.

Replay reason:

- This thread binds the launch-day system to hidden provenance choices.

## Thread 4: The Backroom Shelf

Theme: what the player chooses not to sell becomes a secret history of the store.

Seed unlock:

- Evidence storage exists.
- Player isolates goods or stores evidence.

Pattern unlock:

- Security placeholders become populated.
- Backroom records show cash safe, high-value storage, suspicious goods isolation, or security footage references.
- Multiple isolated items point to the same supplier/customer pattern.

Choices:

- Keep a clean evidence shelf.
- Move goods back to stock.
- Report the whole file.
- Keep records but do nothing.

Consequences:

- Isolation lowers inspection risk.
- Keeping records without action preserves ambiguity.
- Returning goods to stock risks trust.
- Reporting may close supplier paths.

Resolutions:

- The Clean File: evidence is coherent and reportable.
- The Locked Shelf: evidence exists but is never escalated.
- The Vanishing Stock: isolated goods return to the floor and create later consequences.
- The Audit: high inspection risk triggers a forced records evaluation.

Replay reason:

- This thread makes the backroom feel like a moral inventory, not just storage.

## Thread 5: The Collector Network

Theme: loyal collectors become an alternate information network.

Seed unlock:

- Serve collector/preorder customers well.
- Fulfill launch promises.
- Keep rare or clean used goods visible.

Pattern unlock:

- Collectors mention rumors about specific suppliers or impossible stock.
- A regular asks whether you "saved the odd stuff."
- A rare item appears through trade-in or supplier routes.

Choices:

- Protect collector holds.
- Sell rare goods to highest-margin buyers.
- Ask collectors about provenance.
- Refuse questionable collector deals.

Consequences:

- High collector confidence unlocks rare requests.
- Breaking promises damages reputation faster than normal missed sales.
- Collector information can reveal hidden-thread context without formal reporting.

Resolutions:

- Trusted Counter: collectors bring information and business.
- Flipper Shop: high prices and broken holds turn collectors against the store.
- Quiet Network: the player learns supplier patterns through customer trust.
- Locked Out: poor launch and preorder behavior blocks the network.

Replay reason:

- This thread rewards long-term trust over short-term margin.

## Thread 6: The Counterfeit Run

Theme: fake, resealed, or altered goods enter through volume.

Seed unlock:

- Store reaches larger supplier and trade-in volume.
- Product authenticity is uncertain or risk tags include fake, reseal, or serial check.

Pattern unlock:

- More than one customer returns a similar category.
- Supplier terms say no returns.
- Serial and condition mismatches cluster around one source.

Choices:

- Add inspection discipline.
- Discount and sell through.
- Stop buying that source.
- Report counterfeit pattern.
- Isolate all suspect goods.

Consequences:

- Inspection discipline slows growth but protects trust.
- Selling through damages customer trust and inspection risk.
- Reporting may reduce supplier access.
- Stopping a source can create short-term stock shortages.

Resolutions:

- Clean Counter: counterfeit path is contained.
- Bad Batch: reputation takes a hit but the store recovers.
- Known Risk Shop: players who repeatedly sell fakes get a high-risk ending.
- Supplier Reckoning: supplier access changes permanently.

Replay reason:

- This thread tests whether the player can scale without losing standards.

## Thread 7: The Quiet Cash Buyer

Theme: the easiest money in the game feels slightly wrong.

Seed unlock:

- Suspicious contact appears outside the normal queue.
- Dialogue offers cash for bulk goods without receipts.

Pattern unlock:

- Cash buyer references North Dock, Orbit 64 stock, or backroom timing.
- Accepting once creates a follow-up.
- Refusing may close one rumor but protect the store.

Choices:

- Ask for supplier name.
- Refuse the deal.
- Take the cash.
- Document the encounter.
- Report after repeated contact.

Consequences:

- Accepting cash gives immediate money and high risk.
- Asking for a supplier name opens investigation.
- Refusing keeps the store clean.
- Documenting preserves route options.

Resolutions:

- Never Off Register: clean refusal route.
- One Bad Envelope: accepted once, then corrected.
- Cash Habit: repeated accepts create high-risk ending.
- Informant Lead: use the contact to trace supplier issues.

Replay reason:

- This is the cleanest player-facing moral fork.

## Secret Ending Families

These endings layer on top of the business ending.

### Clean Records Ending

Requirements:

- high evidence/documentation
- low risky sales
- low inspection risk
- report or isolate major anomalies

Result:

- store remains trusted
- some supplier access may be lost
- customer trust is strong
- hidden story closes cleanly

### Profitable Silence Ending

Requirements:

- accepted cash offers
- sold anomalous goods
- low documentation
- high cash

Result:

- strong short-term business
- poor records integrity
- high inspection risk
- future operations remain unstable

### Backroom Archivist Ending

Requirements:

- high evidence count
- many isolated goods
- few reports
- medium trust/risk

Result:

- mystery remains partially unresolved
- store survives
- player keeps the file for future years

### Supplier Reckoning Ending

Requirements:

- supplier follow-ups
- suspicious supplier evidence
- report/reject path

Result:

- one supplier path closes or changes
- cleaner access opens later
- short-term stock pain, long-term trust gain

### Collector Confidence Ending

Requirements:

- high launch fulfillment
- high collector trust
- rare goods handled cleanly

Result:

- collector network becomes a premium opportunity path
- hidden rumors become cleaner and more useful

### Audit Ending

Requirements:

- high inspection risk
- repeated risky sales or cash offers
- weak evidence discipline

Result:

- records are evaluated
- cash may be strong, but reputation and access suffer
- store continues, but with consequences

## Secret 100% Across Replays

One save cannot reveal everything. A complete secret-thread player runs multiple stores:

1. Clean Records run.
2. Cash Habit run.
3. Supplier Investigator run.
4. Collector Confidence run.
5. Backroom Archivist run.
6. Pure Retail run that ignores all secrets.

The secret layer is not a checklist in one timeline. It is a replay machine.
